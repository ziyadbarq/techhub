# Keep asset and inventory data clean, connected, and auditable

Barq Assets gives operations, finance, and IT a single source of truth. Reliable records mean faster audits, smoother handoffs, and fewer service interruptions. This page shows how to keep the data healthy.

## What matters for business value

Accurate, linked records reduce loss, speed up procurement, and make audits painless. Focus on the core entities below—each one supports traceability and ownership.

---

### Lifecycle Models & States

**Control how assets move**

Define allowed steps (e.g., Received → In Use → Maintenance → Retired) so every asset follows a predictable path. Clean lifecycle data prevents stranded or "ghost" assets.

- [Models](/lifecycle-models)
- [States](/lifecycle-states)
- [Transitions](/lifecycle-transitions)

---

### Vendors & Contacts

**Who to call, how to renew**

Keep vendor records current to avoid downtime and payment delays. Link contacts so service requests and renewals go to the right people.

- [Vendors](/vendors)
- [Contacts](/vendor-contacts)

---

### Organization & Locations

**Ownership and where things live**

Map assets to org units and locations. Clear ownership speeds approvals and helps audits reconcile stock to physical sites.

- [Organization Units](/organization-units)
- [Locations](/locations)

---

### People & Role Assignments

**Accountability and access**

Tie assets to real people. Keep managers, custodians, and requestors accurate to make lifecycle decisions auditable.

- [Persons](/persons)
- [Role Assignments](/person-role-assignments)

---

### Field Definitions

**Capture what matters**

Configure the custom fields teams need (serials, warranty end, rack position). Good structure keeps forms consistent and reports reliable.

- [Manage fields](/field-definitions)
