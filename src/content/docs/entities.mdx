# Entity Reference

Complete guide to all entities, their fields, relationships, and validation rules.

## Layer 1: Foundation

Core building blocks (11 entities)

### Core Entities

- **OrganizationUnit** - Departments and teams
- **Person** - Employees and contacts
- **Role** - RBAC roles and permissions
- **Location** - Physical and logical locations
- **Vendor** - Suppliers and service providers
- **VendorContact** - Vendor contact persons

### Lifecycle Management

- **LifecycleModel** - Asset lifecycle templates
- **LifecycleState** - Lifecycle states
- **LifecycleTransition** - Allowed state transitions

### Custom Fields

- **FieldDefinition** - Custom field configuration
- **FieldValue** - Custom field data

---

## Layer 2: Asset & Inventory

Asset management and tracking (9 entities)

### Asset Management

- **Asset** - Physical and digital assets
- **AssetCategory** - Asset type classification
- **AssetModel** - Vendor-specific models
- **AssetAssignment** - Asset assignments to people/units

### Maintenance

- **MaintenanceSchedule** - Preventive maintenance plans
- **MaintenanceRecord** - Maintenance work history

### Warranty & Inventory

- **Warranty** - Warranty tracking and expiration
- **StockLocation** - Physical storage locations
- **StockMovement** - Inventory movement tracking
